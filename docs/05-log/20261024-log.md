# Catalog Source Decision Log - 2026-10-24

## Human-approved decision

1. Remove `ownerConsent` from restaurant catalog source files and catalog-build validation.
2. For this educational project, restaurant names, menu names, and prices are treated as factual catalog data and do not require an owner-consent field solely because they identify a restaurant or menu item.
3. Keep source type, capture date, and delivery-markup status so catalog provenance and freshness remain reviewable.
4. Do not commit or publish Grab, Google Maps, LINE MAN, or Wongnai screenshots or images. Catalog descriptions remain team-authored, and menu photos must remain team-owned or appropriately licensed stock images.
5. This decision does not determine or waive third-party platform terms, copyright in creative content, trademark rules, personal-data rules for identifiable people or contact fields, or the separate production-deployment approval gate.

## AI And Google Places Approval

The human project owner approved F20, F21, amended NFR1, NFR13-NFR15, B25, B26, OA2, the strict JSON Gemini response contract, and the corresponding implementation-plan amendment on 2026-10-24.

Approved implementation constraints:

1. The existing catalog remains authoritative; no external restaurant/menu import is added.
2. Existing deterministic rules apply mandatory filters, exclusions, photo eligibility, and restaurant-diverse preselection before Gemini.
3. Gemini receives at most six compact candidates and returns strict JSON with selected candidate IDs and short Thai reasons.
4. Invalid JSON, invalid IDs, timeout, or provider failure uses deterministic fallback. Replacement and pools of three or fewer bypass Gemini.
5. Google Places is fetched only as live evidence for a mapped revealed restaurant. Only `googlePlaceId` is stored; Google photos and live response data are not persisted.
6. Provider credentials remain server-only, and production deployment remains separately gated.

Implementation discovery: `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` returned `404 NOT_FOUND` with a provider message that the model is no longer available to new users. The environment-configurable model was updated to the provider-named replacement, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; cost guards and deterministic fallback remain unchanged.
