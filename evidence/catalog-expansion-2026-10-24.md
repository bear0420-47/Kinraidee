## Photo review and rights

New pools use independent mfu-* keys, so new restaurants do not rotate existing multi-photo pools. Ten new Commons photos and one previously approved Unsplash photo cover 13 menu records (pork and chicken fried rice each reused at two restaurants). Each chosen image was visually inspected, matched by dish and protein, and tested with HTTP 200 image/jpeg. Commons numeric page IDs are real native IDs; no fabricated Openverse IDs. Current Commons thumbnail URLs are retained because rewriting their host produced HTTP 429. New code validates filename/title and page ID/URL and preserves the illustrative label. Openverse search timeouts prevented relying on that API.

All selected Commons images allow commercial reuse and cropping under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Creator, source and license links are in photos.json and generated PHOTO-CREDITS.md / web photoCredits.json. Share-alike terms still apply to adaptations. No recognizable people appear in selected images. No owner permission is claimed. No remote images were committed, no ZIP was created, and no secrets or precise GPS data were retained. Images remain remote links under the existing lifecycle; external availability may change.
