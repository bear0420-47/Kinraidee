## Product interpretation

The evidence supports a constrained decision assistant rather than a broad restaurant directory or an unconstrained random wheel. The primary workflow should accept a few meal-specific conditions, return about three suitable choices, explain them, and allow rapid rejection and replacement without requiring an account.

## Evidence limitations

- The sample contains 31 responses and should not be treated as representative of every potential user.
- Some checkbox questions allowed respondents to select more than the stated maximum; counts are therefore directional rather than rankings with equal response conditions.
- Survey answers establish user pain and preference, not legal applicability or technical feasibility.
- Group voting has weak direct demand and is therefore a Could priority.
