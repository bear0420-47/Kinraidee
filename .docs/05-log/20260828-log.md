## Decisions recorded

1. The primary product is a constrained food-decision assistant, not an unconstrained random wheel or full food-delivery marketplace.
2. The core workflow accepts short conditions and returns up to three suitable choices.
3. General recommendations remain usable without an account or optional personalization consent.
4. Reject-and-replace and session duplicate prevention are Must requirements.
5. One-click randomization is Should because survey support is materially lower than constrained shortlisting.
6. Shared group voting is Could because only 3/31 respondents explicitly requested it.
7. Legal rules that affect the product are represented as numbered `LR-*` requirements in the specification and backlog.
8. Computer Crime Act Section 26 requirements remain Conditional Must pending accountable applicability confirmation.
9. `AGENTS.md` is used as the repository-wide agent instruction file instead of `CLAUDE.md`, per human direction.
10. Raw respondent rows, timestamps, and email addresses are excluded from repository evidence.
