## Form description

You are invited to participate in a user test of Kinraidee, a food decision assistant developed for the Software studycase course at Mae Fah Luang University. This activity is conducted by the Kinraidee team, with Suwaphit Vatidmatee responsible for your personal data. Its purpose is to evaluate the application's main meal-selection workflow and users' acceptance of the system for educational assessment and project improvement. During the test, you will select meal conditions, view suggested meals, try changing an option, and choose a meal. Afterwards, you will answer a questionnaire using a scale of 1 to 5 and may provide comments.

With your consent, we will collect your full name and student ID to identify your participation and maintain a record of your agreement to this educational evaluation. We will also collect your questionnaire responses, meal conditions used during the test, task outcomes, completion times, and notes about difficulties or assistance. Your name and student ID will be kept separately from evaluation responses and linked through a restricted participant code. Only authorized members of the Kinraidee team will have access for the purposes described above. Data will be stored in Google Forms with access restricted to the Kinraidee team until 31 December 2026, after which identifying records will be securely deleted and evaluation data will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized. Reports and presentations will use aggregated or de-identified results without your name or student ID. This consent does not include audio, video, or identifiable screen recording.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline, stop the test, leave questions unanswered, or withdraw your consent without penalty or disadvantage to your grades or relationship with the project team or institution. If you do not agree, please do not enter your name or student ID or submit this study form; you may still use Kinraidee's general meal-selection service. To ask questions, withdraw consent, or request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, contact Suwaphit Vatidmatee at 6631503044@lamduan.mfu.ac.th. Requests will be handled subject to applicable requirements and exceptions. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal. Once data has been irreversibly anonymized, your individual contribution can no longer be identified or removed.

I have read and understood the information above and have had an opportunity to ask questions. I voluntarily agree to participate and consent to the collection, use, and limited disclosure of my personal data for the educational purposes described above. By selecting “I agree,” entering my name below, and submitting this form before the test begins, I confirm my consent. This agreement is separate from any application terms, marketing, or personalization consent.

## Google Forms setup notes — for the team only

- Only the participant completes this form. No facilitator or supervisor signature is required by this draft.
- Present the notice before any identifying fields. Ask participants who do not agree to close the form without submitting their details.
- Turn off automatic email collection and avoid requiring sign-in unless separately justified and explained. Do not request file uploads or additional identifiers.
- Keep the consent form separate from the post-test questionnaire. Assign a participant code after consent and use that code on test records and questionnaire responses; restrict access to the linking record.
- Retain the submission timestamp and the version of the notice shown. Give participants a way to keep or access a copy of that notice without requiring another personal-data field.
- Confirm eligibility, consent-capacity arrangements, and the institution's applicable requirements before recruitment. This draft does not claim ethics approval or legal compliance certification.
